About the Project

Our mission is to democratize high-quality interview preparation using intelligent AI-driven technology.

How it Works

The AI Mock Interview System uses intelligent logic-based analysis to simulate specialized interviewers across various domains. It analyzes your resume content, generates domain-specific interview questions, evaluates user answers, and generates performance reports using advanced evaluation logic.

  • AI-powered resume skill analysis and scoring
  • Domain-specific interview question generation
  • Intelligent answer evaluation with feedback
  • Comprehensive performance reports with strengths & weaknesses

AI Features

Resume Analysis

AI scans your resume for skills, experience, and generates improvement suggestions.

Smart Evaluation

Answers are evaluated using natural language understanding and keyword analysis.

Question Generation

Domain-specific questions are generated based on your selected job role.

Progress Tracking

Track your improvement over multiple sessions with detailed performance insights.

Our Vision

We believe everyone deserves a fair chance at their dream job. By providing a safe, private space to practice, we help candidates build the confidence they need to succeed.
